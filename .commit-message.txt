fix(gigtech): synthesize CustomerSummary when /customers/{cid} also 4xxs

Per-customer service accounts can hit /customers/{cid}/{cloudspaces,audits,
invoices,...} but NOT /customers/{cid} itself — gig.tech apparently treats
the customer-detail endpoint as VCO-level, even when the SA's scope is
that exact customer.

Result was: listCustomers fell back to getCustomer, which also 4xx'd, and
the catch returned []. The operator dashboard rendered with 0 customers,
0 cloudspaces, 0 invoices — even though the data was reachable through
the per-customer drill-downs.

Fix: when getCustomer 4xxs, synthesize a minimal CustomerSummary from
the JWT's azp (customer_id, name, status='active', billable=true) so
the dashboard's customer list has the SA's customer in it. Then
/summary's cloudspaces iterator runs against the real
/customers/{cid}/cloudspaces path and counts come back populated.
