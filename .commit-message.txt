fix(gigtech): tolerate per-customer service-account JWTs

The DynAion service account scoped to abp_technologies_1 works against
/customers/{cid}/* paths but not against the VCO-wide endpoints —
gig.tech's /me 404s with body 'username' (no username claim in the
SA token), and /customers 401/403s (VCO-level enumeration).

Result before this change: the operator console errors out with
"Couldn't load operator data — Not Found: 'username'" because the
dashboard's Promise.all of /v1/vco/{me,customers,locations,...}
propagates the first failure.

Fix: detect service-account tokens by parsing the JWT's `azp` claim
(format `portal-octera-cloud.customers.{cid}.service_accounts.{name}`)
and gracefully degrade three calls:

 - getMe: on 404 → synthesize a Me with vco_name="Octera (scoped to
   {cid})", customers=[{customer_id: cid, name: cid}], so the
   identity card and customer list still render.
 - listCustomers: on 4xx → fall back to fetching just the SA's own
   customer via /customers/{cid} and return as a 1-element list.
 - listLocations: on 4xx → return empty array; the dashboard handles
   "No locations available" already.

Once a partner-level credential lands the fallbacks become dead code —
the real /me and /customers responses come back. Detection is fully
contained in detectServiceAccount() at the top of the file.
